Our mission

About HOL Foundation

At HOL Foundation, we believe in the power of education, health, and addressing the basic needs of individuals. Our dedicated team is driven by a shared vision to foster prosperity and create a brighter future. We are committed to breaking down barriers and creating an inclusive society where everyone has equal opportunities to thrive.

In our pursuit of excellence, we have recently taken a significant stride by adopting a government school and pioneering the Lyari Entrepreneurship Academy. This groundbreaking initiative provides a platform for students, including single mothers and youth, to embark on a journey of free education in the dynamic fields of IT and Start-ups. We empower them with the knowledge and skills necessary to navigate the ever-changing world, fostering innovation and economic growth within the community.
