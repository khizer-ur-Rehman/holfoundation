Our journey · Lyari, Karachi

Rooted in Lyari.
Growing through learning.

HOL Foundation began in 2014 as a voluntary community initiative in Lyari, Karachi, focused on education, youth empowerment and community development.

In July 2025, after years of community service, HOL Foundation Pakistan was formally registered—strengthening its commitment to sustainable development and social change.
