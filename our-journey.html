Our journey · Lyari, Karachi

Rooted in Lyari.
Growing through learning.

House of Learning Foundation Pakistan began its journey in 2014 as a voluntary community initiative in Lyari, Karachi, dedicated to education, youth empowerment, social welfare and community development. For over a decade, we worked tirelessly on the ground to create opportunities for underprivileged children and families through free educational and community programs.

After years of impactful service and community engagement, House of Learning Foundation Pakistan was formally registered in July 2025, strengthening its commitment to sustainable development and expanding its mission of empowering communities through education and social change.

Milestones
2026Learning partnerships and digital opportunity

Literacy & Non-Formal Education, Sindh

In 2026, HOL Foundation Pakistan signed a Memorandum of Understanding with the Directorate of Literacy & Non-Formal Education Sindh, under the School Education & Literacy Department, Government of Sindh. The partnership strengthens non-formal and accelerated learning pathways for out-of-school children in District South and District West (Orangi Town).

Bahria University Karachi Campus

In 2026, HOL Foundation Pakistan signed an MoU with Bahria University Karachi Campus, laying the foundation for a meaningful collaboration in learning, innovation and social impact.

10,000 students trained

In 2026, HOL Foundation Pakistan proudly reached a major milestone—successfully training 10,000 students through its IT education project and expanding digital access and future opportunities for youth.

2025Recognition for community-led change

inDrive Changemakers

In 2025, inDrive celebrated five inspiring changemakers across Pakistan—including Irfan Junejo—and HOL is proud that Hunain Abbas, a founder from the streets of Lyari, was among them. His portrait now shines on a billboard at DHA Phase 8 near Creek Walk, showing how courage and consistency can transform lives. HOL is grateful to inDrive Pakistan for believing in community-led change.

K-Electric Karachi Award

HOL Foundation Pakistan once again proudly received the K-Electric Karachi Award, a continued recognition of its powerful impact in uplifting communities.

2024New spaces to learn, grow and thrive

IT Vocational Center

In 2024, the IT Vocational Center was inaugurated at the HOL Government Adopted School in Lyari with generous support from Rotary Club of Karachi Sunset Millennium, opening new digital learning pathways for students.

HOL Karate Club

In 2024, HOL Foundation Pakistan launched the HOL Karate Club for the community, promoting discipline, confidence and physical strength among youth.

K-Electric Karachi Award

In 2024, HOL Foundation Pakistan proudly received the K-Electric Karachi Award, recognizing its impactful work in education and community development.

HOL Preschool

In 2024, HOL Foundation Pakistan established its first HOL Preschool, based on the Kiran Satellite model, at the adopted government school—bringing quality early education into the public sector.

HMR Waterfront

In 2024, HOL Foundation students shone at the HMR Waterfront event with Margalla Trail Runners, sponsored by inDrive, showcasing determination, sportsmanship and the rising spirit of young talent.

German Consulate General Karachi

In 2024, Dr. Lotz from the German Consulate General Karachi visited the HOL Government School Campus three times, recognizing and encouraging the educational work being done for the community.

New learning journeys

In 2024, HOL Ambassadors—the future leaders of HOL Foundation Pakistan—began new journeys at Pingu’s English School Pakistan and Kiran Foundation DCTO, reflecting the resilience, growth and potential of Lyari’s youth.

2023A government school joins the journey

HOL adopts a government school

In 2023, HOL Foundation Pakistan expanded its mission by adopting a government school in Lyari, bringing quality education closer to the community.

2022Lyari Entrepreneur Academy

Lyari Entrepreneur Academy

In 2022, the Lyari Entrepreneur Academy was established, creating a platform to nurture skills, leadership and entrepreneurship among local youth.

2021The first steps in digital learning

First IT batch

In 2021, HOL Foundation Pakistan launched its very first IT batch with just two laptops, sparking a digital transformation journey for the youth of Lyari.

Dosti Project

In 2021, HOL Foundation Pakistan signed an MoU with Kiran Foundation under the Dosti Project, strengthening collaboration to expand educational impact.

2020Learning and enterprise for women and children

Women Entrepreneurship Program

In 2020, alongside the preschool initiative, Hunain Abbas launched HOL's first Women Entrepreneurship Program at NJV School, adopted by Akhuwat Foundation, empowering women toward financial independence.

Early childhood education

In 2020, a formal preschool was launched under the leadership of Areeba Hunain, laying strong foundations for early childhood education for 200 children.

2019A structured school takes shape

Structured Non-Formal School

In 2019, founders Hunain Abbas and Areeba Yaqoob established a structured non-formal school, opening doors of learning to 50 students.

2018Local youth come together

HOL Community Center

In 2018, the first HOL Community Center in Lyari brought local youth together to teach and uplift street children through the power of education.

2016A dream begins in one small room

Changing lives through education

Started in 2016 from one small room, HOL Foundation began with Hunain's dream at an adult literacy center—and grew into a movement changing lives through education.
