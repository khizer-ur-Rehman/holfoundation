Lyari Entrepreneur Academy

Free education in IT and start-ups, opening pathways for youth, students and single mothers.

Skills to shape what comes next.

Lyari Entrepreneur Academy (LEE) gives students, single mothers and young people a platform to learn in the dynamic fields of IT and start-ups. The program equips learners with knowledge and skills to navigate a changing world, encourage innovation and contribute to economic growth in the community.

More than 5,000 youth, single mothers and students have benefited from LEE, and 50+ alumni have found jobs in reputable organizations.
