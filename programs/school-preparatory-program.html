HOL School Preparatory Program

Helping children reconnect with education and prepare to thrive in school.

A bridge back to the classroom.

Many children face barriers to entering formal education. HOL’s School Preparatory Program supports out-of-school children as they prepare to join school and continue learning.

Through this work, more than 500 out-of-school children have been successfully admitted to school.
