At HOL Foundation, we believe education, health and essential support can help every person move forward.

Our team works to remove barriers and build an inclusive community where people have room to learn, grow and thrive. We believe in equality, collaboration and the power of opportunity shared.

In Lyari, we adopted a government school and established the Lyari Entrepreneurship Academy. Together, these initiatives connect students, young people and single mothers with education, IT and start-up skills—building confidence and new possibilities for the future.

FOUNDER’S INTRODUCTION LYARI, KARACHI
H
Founder · Community leader

Hunain Abbas

Building opportunity through education, service and local leadership.

Hunain began his journey at 16, starting street schools in Lyari. A belief in education and community-led progress has guided his work ever since.

He later adopted a government school and established the Lyari Entrepreneurship Academy—the first entrepreneurship academy in the area—creating free learning opportunities in IT and start-ups for youth and single mothers. His vision is a Lyari where everyone can access quality education and shape a prosperous future.

His professional experience includes HR relations, an affiliation with the Navy League, and work with Feroze1888, Fulcrum Pvt Ltd, Al-Sons, HRSI and Borque Cooperation. He has also served as President of the Rotaract Club of Karachi Sunset Millennium, supporting young people to build skills and take part in social initiatives.
