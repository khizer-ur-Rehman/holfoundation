Lasting change begins by listening to the people who know their community best.

At HOL Foundation, we believe education, health and essential support can help every person move forward.

Our team works to remove barriers and build an inclusive community where people have room to learn, grow and thrive. We believe in equality, collaboration and the power of opportunity shared.

In Lyari, we adopted a government school and established the Lyari Entrepreneurship Academy. Together, these initiatives connect students, young people and single mothers with education, IT and start-up skills—building confidence and new possibilities for the future.

FOUNDER’S INTRODUCTION
HOL FOUNDATION · COMMUNITY LEADERSHIP

Hunain Abbas

At 16, Hunain started street schools in Lyari. That first step grew into a lasting commitment to education and community-led change.

He later adopted a government school and established the area’s first Entrepreneurship Academy. Through the Lyari Entrepreneurship Academy, young people and single mothers can access free IT and start-up learning.

His experience spans HR relations, the Navy League, Feroze1888, Fulcrum Pvt Ltd, Al-Sons, HRSI and Borque Cooperation. As President of the Rotaract Club of Karachi Sunset Millennium, he has encouraged young people to build skills and serve their community.

HIS VISION FOR LYARI

Everyone can access quality education and build a more prosperous future.
